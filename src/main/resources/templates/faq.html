Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions about SkillHub.

Getting Started

How do I create an account on SkillHub?
Click the "Sign Up" button on the top-right corner. You can register with your email address or use social login. Once registered, you can start browsing and enrolling in courses immediately.
Is SkillHub free to use?
SkillHub offers both free and paid courses. Many instructors provide free content, while premium courses come at various price points. Browse our catalog to find courses that fit your budget.
What devices can I use to access SkillHub?
SkillHub works on any device with a web browser — desktop, laptop, tablet, or mobile phone. Our responsive design ensures a great learning experience on any screen size.

Courses & Learning

How long do I have access to a purchased course?
Once you enroll in a course, you have lifetime access. Learn at your own pace, revisit content anytime, and benefit from future updates the instructor makes to the course.
Do I earn a certificate after completing a course?
Yes! Upon completing all lectures and passing the course exam, you receive an official SkillHub certificate. You can download it as a PDF and share it on LinkedIn or add it to your resume.
Can I download course videos for offline viewing?
Currently, courses are available for streaming only. However, you can take notes within the platform which are saved to your profile and can be downloaded for offline reference.

Payments & Refunds

What payment methods are accepted?
We accept credit cards, debit cards, UPI, net banking, and major digital wallets. All payments are processed securely through Razorpay, our trusted payment gateway.
What is the refund policy?
SkillHub offers a 30-day refund policy for most course purchases. If you're not satisfied with a course, you can request a full refund within 30 days of enrollment by contacting our support team.

Teaching on SkillHub

How can I become an instructor?
Register as an instructor during sign-up or update your role in your profile. Once approved, you can create courses, upload videos, set pricing, and start earning from your expertise.
How do instructors get paid?
Instructors earn revenue from each course sale. Payments are processed monthly and deposited directly into your linked bank account. You can track your earnings in the instructor dashboard.
